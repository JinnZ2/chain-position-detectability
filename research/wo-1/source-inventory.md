# WO-1 Source Inventory — OWASP Agentic AI Control and Risk Materials

**Purpose.** This inventory establishes the source baseline and audit-unit boundaries for WO-1. It is a source-and-scope record only. It makes **no finding**, score, compliance determination, or inference about the subject under review.

**Research cut-off:** 19 September 2026.
**Audit-unit rule:** A unit includes every normative keyword obligation in its stated boundary, including conditional **MUST**, **MUST NOT**, **SHOULD**, **SHOULD NOT**, **MAY**, and **REQUIRED** language, together with any incorporated JSON Schema constraint. The fifteen units below are deliberately adjacent-clause groupings rather than a dilution of the source requirements.

## Authoritative current materials

The current OWASP material set separates **runtime-control conformance** from **agentic-risk guidance**. The enforceable, clause-level control baseline is the **Agent Control Standard (ACS) v0.1.0** specification and its conformance profiles. Its project repository currently carries a project version marker of `0.1.2` and a `v0.1.1` release tag, but the formal normative Instrument document and all published schema identifiers remain explicitly versioned **v0.1.0**. Accordingly, this inventory audits the v0.1.0 specification clauses, not the repository marker as though it were a newer specification edition.[4] [5] [6]

The **OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications 2026** is the current OWASP Agentic Security Initiative risk framework. Its title page states “Version 2026 — December 2025”; the official OWASP resource page is dated 9 December 2025, while the retained original PDF metadata is dated 10 December 2025. This one-day display/metadata difference does not create a competing edition. The Top 10 is a peer-reviewed, practical risk and mitigation guide, not a formal conformance specification; its prevention-and-mitigation language is therefore recorded as **explanatory guidance**, not converted into normative pass/fail clauses.[1] [2] [3]

## Completeness and boundary notes

**Included normative surface.** ACS-01 through ACS-10 cover the normative material in the Instrument specification, Conformance Profiles, Hook/MCP extension, Provenance and Intent concepts, Trace Events/OTel extension, Inspect/AgBOM specification, and the v0.1.0 schemas incorporated by those documents. They include baseline ACS-Core and every optional profile as a conditional audit population. The profile claim itself matters: a deployment that does not claim a profile is not audited as though it claimed that profile, except where a separate requirement makes the profile mandatory for the chosen policy mode (for example, policy depending on inventory or provenance).[6] [7] [13]

**Intentionally excluded as non-obligatory.** Instrument §14 expressly supplies no v0.1 multi-tenancy isolation rules. Instrument §15 is labelled “Out of Scope (Deferred)” and §16 is a roadmap. They contain no v0.1 conformance clauses and therefore are not silently omitted audit requirements. Similarly, `protocols/A2A/*` wrapping is reserved but deferred to v0.2; it is not an ACS v0.1 audit failure if absent.[6]

**Interpretation boundary.** The Top 10 remains essential context for mapping observed conditions to agentic risks. It is not used to manufacture requirements that its text characterizes as practical or prevention guidance. Conversely, ACS is a wire-format/control specification: a later clause-level audit should cite the relevant ACS unit, individual clause, incorporated schema, declared profile, and observed evidence before reaching any conclusion.
