## Selected ecology synthesis

The Wiley full-text record for Jiang et al., “Global patterns and drivers of plant–soil microbe interactions,” identifies the article as a 2024 *Ecology Letters* synthesis. Its abstract states that the corpus contains **5,969 plant–soil-feedback observations from 202 studies**. The page identifies field and greenhouse experimental environments and reports that the synthesis examined experimental choices during conditioning and feedback phases.

Source: <https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/ele.14364>

The Dryad dataset API confirms title *Dataset of global plant-soil feedback*, DOI `10.5061/dryad.n2z34tn35`, public version 6, publication date 18 December 2023, public visibility, and CC0 1.0 Universal licensing. It describes the same 5,969 observations from 202 studies.

Source: <https://datadryad.org/api/v2/datasets/doi%3A10.5061%2Fdryad.n2z34tn35>

## CR2/Satterthwaite sensitivity

Pustejovsky and Tipton describe bias-reduced linearization for cluster-robust variance estimation with Satterthwaite approximate degrees of freedom. Their abstract warns that conventional cluster-robust Wald tests can over-reject with few clusters and reports that the proposed small-sample method improves Type I error in simulations. WO-5 uses this method only as a sensitivity analysis; the primary CR1 estimate is retained and labeled as a conventional approximation.

Source: <https://doi.org/10.1080/07350015.2016.1247004>
