# WO-5 validation synthesis: H1–H2 plant–soil-feedback contrast

**Author:** Manus AI
**Date:** 19 September 2026
**Scope:** Synthesis of the independent statistical, ecological-methods, and reproducibility validations of the WO-5 categorical H1–H2 analysis.

**Audit-trail status:** This document records the pre-correction validation findings. The corrected release replaces the randomization, adds CR2/Satterthwaite sensitivity inference, repairs documentation, completes the candidate inventory, pins the runtime, and publishes a conservative partial source crosswalk. See the [correction register](correction-register.md) and [final report](final-report.md). Irreducible design and provenance limitations remain.

## Overall verdict

**The primary numerical result is computationally reproducible and may be published as a narrowly framed associational result after the corrections below.** Independent reimplementation reproduced the primary effect-level H2-minus-H1 estimate to numerical precision, and an execution audit reproduced all supplied outputs bit-for-bit in the audited environment. No validator identified a material arithmetic or primary-model implementation error. [1] [2]

Publication must not characterize this result as **information loss, pre-entry loss, hop-caused degradation, ecological realism, retained information, or a within-source carry effect**. The estimand is a difference in average **signed individual plant–soil-feedback (PSF) log-response ratio (LRR)** between two metadata-defined design groups in a selected subset: field-conditioned material with field feedback (H1) versus field-conditioned material with greenhouse feedback (H2). The public metadata do not prove every focal organism’s field origin or document each transfer event in primary-study prose. [3] [5]

The numerical result is publishable only with three qualifications presented alongside it. First, it is an association that is confounded by feedback environment and markedly different source-study and experimental compositions. Second, the confidence interval does **not** establish practical equivalence. Third, the study is computationally reproducible from the supplied coded file but is **not independently time-verified as an end-to-end preregistered workflow** under the current archival record. [1] [2] [3]

## Validated primary finding and publication wording

The preregistered categorical fallback was appropriate because only H1 and H2 met the established category threshold; no three-category ordinal model was fit. The effect-level random-effects meta-regression retained 1,523 effects: 326 H1 effects from 23 sources and 1,197 H2 effects from 91 sources. The union has 111 sources because three sources contain both categories. The primary H2-minus-H1 estimate is **−0.005277 LRR** (source-clustered CR1 SE **0.092398**; *t*(110) = −0.0571; 95% CI **−0.188388 to 0.177834**; *p* = **0.9546**; row-level residual heterogeneity under the diagonal working model, \(\tau^2\), = **0.363775**). [1] [6]

A publishable result statement is:

> In the metadata-resolved subset of field-conditioned plant–soil-feedback studies, we found no detectable difference in average **signed individual-PSF log-response ratio** between field-feedback (H1) and greenhouse-feedback (H2) designs (H2 − H1 = −0.0053 LRR, 95% source-clustered CR1 CI −0.1884 to 0.1778; *p* = 0.955). This interval is not contained within the prespecified ±log(1.10) equivalence margin, so the analysis does not establish practical equivalence. Because the category definition incorporates feedback setting and the two categories have different study and experimental compositions, this is an associational comparison and cannot be interpreted as information loss, a causal effect of hop distance, ecological realism, or degradation of a common field signal. H2 denotes field-conditioned biological material or soil followed by greenhouse feedback; it does not verify individual-level field-origin history. [1] [3] [5]

The scope condition belongs in the same abstract, conclusion, or results paragraph: **4,446 of 5,969 effects (74.5%) and 91 HU-only source studies were excluded** under the conservative unresolved-category rule. The result therefore generalizes only to the metadata-resolved field-conditioning subset, not to the full PSF corpus or to greenhouse-conditioned experiments whose history could not be resolved. [3] [5] [6]

| **Irreducible design and data limitations** | Only three sources contain both H1 and H2. Twenty sources are H1-only and 88 are H2-only, so the identified rank-two primary model is predominantly a **between-source** comparison. The paired sensitivity is based on three sources and is highly imprecise. [1] [3] | State this overlap structure prominently. Do not present the estimate as a within-source contrast, source-fixed-effect estimate, or causal comparison. |
| **Irreducible design and data limitations** | H1 is field-conditioned/field-feedback and H2 is field-conditioned/greenhouse-feedback. The groups also differ in PSF comparator, soil and inoculum treatment, taxon, geography, era, and source-study portfolio. Field and greenhouse PSF assays can differ biologically through abiotic variation, pot/root confinement, enemies and macrofauna, soil handling, inoculum dilution, competition, colonization, and duration. [3] | Clustered inference, aggregation, leave-one-out refitting, and permutation do not identify a hop effect or eliminate composition confounding. The result must remain an association between setting-defined study groups. |
| **Irreducible design and data limitations** | Shared controls and other within-source sampling covariances are unavailable. The likelihood and source-category cell weights therefore use a diagonal working covariance; clustering the final coefficient covariance does not repair the \(\tau^2\) estimate or weights. [1] | Describe row-level \(\tau^2\) as **residual effect heterogeneity under a diagonal working covariance**, not unqualified between-study variance. |
| **Irreducible estimand limitation** | Signed mean LRR can conceal opposing ecological changes. The dataset has no direct measure of information fidelity, detectability, signal-to-noise, prediction error, or agreement with a field reference. [3] | The result cannot test information retention or loss, even if the numerical contrast remains near zero. |

## Sensitivity results that support, but do not enlarge, the claim

The source-category aggregation gives H2 minus H1 = **−0.002814** (CR1 SE 0.092804; 95% CI −0.186729 to 0.181102; *p* = 0.9759; \(\tau^2\) = 0.203423) across 114 source-category cells from 111 sources. Equal source-category weighting gives 0.0234 (95% CI −0.1652 to 0.2120; *p* = 0.8061). Leave-one-source-out source-aggregated estimates range from −0.0426 to 0.0337, and every refitted interval includes zero. These checks show that no single source generates the observed near-zero unpaired contrast; they do **not** make the H1 and H2 study portfolios exchangeable. [1] [6]

The within-source paired sensitivity is based on three sources and estimates 0.2448 (95% CI −0.5560 to 1.0455; *p* = 0.319). It is non-informative about a common within-source effect rather than confirmation that such an effect is absent. [1] [3] [6]

The practical-equivalence result is also validated: the primary 95% CI is not inside ±log(1.10) = ±0.095310. The independently calculated conventional 90% CI is −0.158549 to 0.147995, and the two-one-sided-test result is *p* = 0.1660. Thus, “no detectable difference” is warranted; “equivalent,” “no association,” and “no meaningful difference” are not. [1]

## What the validation does and does not establish

The three validations agree that the supplied data, code, and primary calculation support a reproducible **fixed-input numerical association**. They do not all support the stronger surrounding labels that could be inferred from the project’s original framing. In particular, the codebook’s statement that it was preregistered before corpus discovery remains a claim in the current record rather than an independently authenticated chronology, and H2 remains a metadata-supported phase-setting proxy rather than a verified individual-provenance or transfer record. [3] [4] [5]

Accordingly, the result should be released as **computationally reproducible with material archival/preregistration qualifications**, accompanied by the corrected permutation description or calculation, repaired documentation, and the exact scope and interpretation language above. The core number may appear in a paper or report; the present evidence does not support promoting it into a causal carry-test conclusion or an end-to-end independently verified preregistration claim. [1] [2] [3] [4]
