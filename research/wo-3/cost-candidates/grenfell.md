# WO-3 cost-boundary evidence record — Grenfell Tower: zinc-to-PE-ACM rainscreen decision

**Candidate ID:** `grenfell-zinc-to-pe-acm-rainscreen`
**Strict five-gate result:** **PARTIAL / NON-QUALIFYING**
**Decision period:** January 2013–September 2014
**Declared observation horizon (`H`):** January 2013 through the 2023 civil-settlement hearing/reporting date. Later publications are used as evidence about events within that horizon; they do not extend it.
**Currency convention:** Amounts are nominal pound sterling (£) in the stated historical period. No inflation adjustment, currency conversion, or monetisation of death, injury, bereavement, or trauma is made.

## Finding

**OBSERVED.** The final 30 January 2014 National Building Specification (NBS) named Proteus HR zinc honeycomb rainscreen panels and invited aluminium composite material (ACM) alternatives. The statutory Grenfell Tower Inquiry records value-engineering pressure to replace zinc with cheaper material, Rydon's 20 March 2014 cassette-fixed ACM figure of **£293,368**, and the subsequent selection of cassette-form Reynobond 55 ACM with an unmodified polyethylene (PE) core. It records planning approval for cassette-fixed ACM on 31 July 2014 and formal permission on 25 September 2014.[1]

**OBSERVED.** The Inquiry found that using cassette-form ACM panels with unmodified PE cores as the rainscreen was “primarily responsible for the rapid spread of the fire.” The fire escaped Flat 16 into the external envelope, spread rapidly over the building, and killed 72 people.[1] [2]

**DERIVED.** These facts establish a documented cost-led zinc-to-PE-ACM substitution and an authoritative causal finding about the realised PE-ACM selection. They do **not** establish that the exact zinc baseline used in Rydon's comparison was a demonstrated fire-safety control, or that retaining it would have prevented rapid spread, every death, or any stated downstream cost. Under the preregistered strict rule, gates 1 and 4 therefore fail. **This candidate does not qualify as a WO-3 causal cost case.**

**UNRESOLVED.** The decision record does not identify Rydon's costed “Zinc (cassette-fixed)” baseline as the distinct Rheinzink panel that the Inquiry describes as not combustible. It also does not provide a contemporaneous, project-specific certification of the precise Proteus HR configuration used as that cost baseline. The record preserves this product-identity gap rather than converting a plausible counterfactual into a finding.

## Status and interpretation key

**OBSERVED** denotes a fact, amount, document, or finding directly supported by a cited source. **DERIVED** denotes an inference limited to the cited facts and the declared boundary. **MODELLED / CONDITIONAL** denotes a proposition that would require a stated counterfactual or configuration assumption. **PLANNED** denotes a proposed evidence-gathering or reassessment step, not an observed outcome. **UNRESOLVED** denotes a material question for which the current record supplies no adequate answer.

## Decision and cost record

### The proposed control and the product-evidence conflict

**OBSERVED.** The proposed control is narrowly framed as retaining the zinc rainscreen baseline rather than substituting a PE-core ACM rainscreen. The final NBS specified Proteus HR zinc honeycomb panels while bidders were asked to price ACM alternatives. Separately, the Inquiry records that an earlier Rheinzink panel was not combustible.[1]

**UNRESOLVED.** These are not interchangeable product findings. A contemporaneous September 2014 Proteus HR brochure describes the standard system as achieving **Class 0** under BS 476 Parts 6 and 7. It describes an A2 system as non-standard and separately available. A later September 2019 Proteus overview instead says the system achieved **A2-s1,d0 with the majority of facing materials**.[7] [8] The sources may reflect a product revision, different configurations, or different testing and classification routes. The available evidence does not resolve that conflict, tie either description to Grenfell's exact configuration, or tie either description to Rydon's priced zinc baseline. The conflict reinforces the failure of gate 1.

**MODELLED / CONDITIONAL.** Even a future certificate identifying an exact zinc configuration would be empirical certification under its stated test conditions. It would not, by itself, prove the model-conditional but-for claim that retaining that configuration would have prevented all rapid spread, deaths, or costs. It would also not establish zero crossings under the WO-3 ontology.

### The cost-led decision process

**OBSERVED.** In January 2013, Leadbitter proposed aluminium panels in place of zinc at an estimated **£250,000** saving, later costed at **£300,000**. Studio E later described replacing zinc with a cheaper material as an obvious value-engineering possibility while the Tenant Management Organisation (TMO) sought a cheaper alternative. The final NBS retained zinc but requested ACM prices. By March 2014, the rainscreen was a stated source of saving. The Inquiry found that Rydon had a financial interest in promoting ACM and that the TMO did not receive the full information about the available saving.[1]

**OBSERVED.** Harley's 14 March 2014 presentation gave savings of **£419,627** for cassette-fixed ACM and **£576,973** for riveted ACM. Rydon's 20 March presentation gave **£293,368** for cassette-fixed ACM and **£376,175** for riveted ACM.[1] From March 2014, Reynobond 55 PE was the sole proposed product. The later approvals implemented the cassette-fixed ACM choice.[1]

| Contemporaneous presentation | Figure | Permitted interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Leadbitter, January/February 2013 | £250,000; £300,000 | Earlier aluminium-for-zinc estimates that evidence cost pressure. |
| Harley, 14 March 2014 | £419,627 cassette-fixed; £576,973 riveted | Different fixing options and commercial presentation. |
| Rydon, 20 March 2014 | **£293,368 cassette-fixed; £376,175 riveted** | The selected record's decision-boundary figure is the cassette-fixed **£293,368** saving. |

**DERIVED.** The amounts are competing or alternative commercial estimates, not components of a single cost. They must not be added, substituted for one another, or described as the installed-cost differential between a proven safety control and the final system. This record uses **£293,368** only as the contemporaneous saving stated for Rydon's cassette-fixed ACM option.[1]

## Harm connection and its limit

**OBSERVED.** The Inquiry states:

> “it was the decision to use aluminium composite panels with unmodified polyethylene cores in what was known as ‘cassette’ form as the rainscreen that was primarily responsible for the rapid spread of the fire.” [1]

**OBSERVED.** The Inquiry records that cost, appearance, and programme were considered while fire performance was not adequately included in the relevant decision process. It also identifies other contributing matters, including combustible insulation, deficiencies in the fire strategy and processes, and response failures.[1]

**DERIVED.** The supported chain is: cost pressure and a zinc-to-PE-ACM design substitution; selection and installation of cassette-form PE ACM; rapid external fire spread for which that selection was primarily responsible; and later emergency, recovery, litigation, and remediation burdens. The record does **not** shorten this into “£293,368 caused £X,” nor into a claim that zinc alone would have prevented all harm.

This is a null result bounded to this candidate, the stated sources, and `H`; it is not evidence of zero harm, zero downstream cost, or zero responsibility.

## Crossing record under the WO-3 metric

**OBSERVED.** **System `S`** is the whole Grenfell refurbishment system, including the 2013–16 decision and completed external-wall system. **Boundary `B`** is the stipulated procurement/accounting boundary that represents the cassette-ACM saving as the relevant project cost. **Horizon `H`** runs from January 2013 through the 2023 civil-settlement hearing/reporting date. The relevant phases are construction, operation, and post-fire recovery. The 4 September 2024 Inquiry report is an authoritative reconstruction used as evidence; it is not itself an event that extends `H`.[1] [11]

**OBSERVED.** The RBKC gross-revenue-spend category supplies one supported positive witness outside `B`: a human/institutional burden associated with the whole refurbishment system. At minimum, this establishes `L_human/institutional(B,H) > 0` in the stated currency category. The witness is not a material-specific allocation, a complete crossing vector, or a monetary crossing rate.[3]

**DERIVED.** For this stipulated `B` and finite `H`, the **whole refurbishment system is nonterminal** because one supported positive human/institutional witness is sufficient. This classification applies to `S`, not to the isolated zinc-to-PE-ACM substitution. It does not quantify a cost share or establish crossings in other media.

**UNRESOLVED.** Matter, energy and momentum, information and control, biological, and dependency/exposure channels have not been independently quantified or cited as positive lower-bound witnesses in this record. They are therefore not claimed as additional positive channels. No complete crossing vector or channel-specific rate is reported.

**UNRESOLVED — exact-zero ontology.** This evidence record establishes neither `U_m = 0` nor any all-channel exact-zero upper bound. It makes no claim that no crossing was detected, that all listed channels lie below stated detection bounds, or that every possible crossing is exactly zero for all time. The nonterminal result is limited to one observed institutional witness in the declared finite frame; it is not a universal physical claim.

**MODELLED / CONDITIONAL.** A manufacturer test or certificate, if the exact configuration were identified, would be an empirical certification under specified conditions. It would not establish an exact-zero crossing vector or the counterfactual prevention of every loss.

**PLANNED.** This case does not assess an asymptote. Accordingly, no asymptotic nonterminal classification, theoretical physical-model result, or “positive crossing after correction” asymptote test is applicable here. Any future asymptote assessment must state its own system, boundary, horizon, evidence status, and supported positive witness.

## Remaining evidence gaps and falsifiers

**UNRESOLVED.** A contemporaneous certificate, technical sheet, tender schedule, or other primary record is needed to identify the exact Rydon costed zinc baseline and its installed configuration. Without it, gate 1 remains not met.

**UNRESOLVED.** No authoritative source in this record makes the control-specific but-for finding required by gate 4. Evidence that the exact retained configuration would have prevented rapid spread would be needed to reassess that gate; it would still not prove prevention of all deaths, losses, or crossing categories.

**UNRESOLVED.** No source apportions Council spending, grants, settlements, or national programmes to the zinc-to-PE-ACM substitution. Any calculation that divides £134.803m, £150m, or national funding commitments by £293,368 is unsupported and rejected.

**PLANNED.** Reassessment is warranted only if the missing exact-product evidence and a control-specific authoritative causal analysis are located. The revised assessment must retain separate cost categories, a finite declared horizon, and the prohibition on cost aggregation and ratio claims.
