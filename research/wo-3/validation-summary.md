# WO-3 Validation Synthesis: Terminal-System Boundary and Cost Cases

**Validation-time status:** **Needs correction; not publication-ready.** This synthesis preserves the independent pre-reconciliation findings. Its required corrections have been applied to the evidence records, [`workflow-synthesis.md`](workflow-synthesis.md), and [`final-report.md`](final-report.md). The document remains unchanged in substance as an audit trail and does not describe the final publication status.

## 1. Overall disposition

The central ontology conclusion **survives only in its narrowed form**. The reviewed record supports the proposition that **finite empirical work can establish a detection, or a scoped non-detection and upper bound, but cannot empirically certify exact zero crossing across every medium for all time for a real system**. That conclusion is **derived methodological reasoning**, not an observed result supplied by a method-detection-limit standard. A finite-horizon internal recorder can later support a bounded claim such as “no specified crossing was detected during the named horizon above the stated detection bound.” It cannot certify universal all-time/all-medium zero, whole-life terminality, or completeness of the channel inventory. Exact zero can be established only as a **model-conditional proof** after stipulating the system, boundary, inventory, laws, parameters, and initial conditions.[1] [2] [3]

All **three selected asymptotes remain nonterminal after correction**, but they do so with different evidentiary statuses. Forsmark is nonterminal on an observed construction/institutional witness; Voyager 1 is empirically nonterminal across its historical whole life and only **model-conditionally** nonterminal in the stipulated future post-radio scenario; and GW150914 is nonterminal only **conditional on the general-relativistic source-attribution and propagation model**. None supplies model-free empirical certification of a remote, invented boundary crossing or an exact-zero proposition.[4] [5] [6] [7]

**None of the six cost candidates qualifies under all five preregistered gates.** Deepwater Horizon, Flint, Boeing 737 MAX, Challenger, and Texas fail the dispositive contemporaneous same-control cost/rationale gate; Texas also lacks a documented identified actor’s decision to decline the defined control. Grenfell fails the control-specific availability and causation gates. This is a bounded selection null, not evidence that no further documents could ever change an individual record. No downstream total, cost ratio, cross-control transfer, or but-for allocation is validated.[1] [8] [9] [10] [11] [12] [13]

> **Publication rule:** Every correction below is required. Until the evidence labels, scope, source conflicts, gate results, horizons, and calculations are changed as stated, the integrated record must not be labelled publication-ready.

## 2. Asymptote dispositions

### 2.1 Forsmark KBS-3 repository — **nonterminal on observed preparation and institutional interaction**

Forsmark remains nonterminal within the declared whole-life accounting frame. The decisive witness is not a present radionuclide release, observed underground rock removal, or a projected heat flow. It is the observed January 2025 ground-breaking and preparatory surface activity, supplemented where necessary by observed regulatory, information, and institutional interfaces. This is an accounting classification only; it neither establishes repository unsafety nor contradicts a model-conditional safety case.[4] [14] [15]

The published account must distinguish those observed surface works from underground excavation. SKB’s licensing material says that spent-fuel-repository rock excavation required Swedish Radiation Safety Authority approval. It must also disclose the unresolved first-party volume conflict: the principal SKB project page reports about **2.7 million m³**, while SKB International reports about **2.3 million m³**. Neither is an observed cumulative excavation total.[4] [14]

The **230,000 m³/year** figure must be deleted as a planned construction-excavation rate. Although 2.3 million divided by ten is arithmetically correct, the ten years refer to the period before disposal may begin, whereas rock excavation and tunnel development are described as continuing over several decades. Likewise, the 2.30 m³/year million-year quotient is permissible only as an explicitly hypothetical calculation with an assumed closure date; the alternative official total would yield roughly 2.70 m³/year under the same assumption.[4] [14]

The 10.2 MW and 2.25321264 × 10^16 J calculations remain correct only as **counterfactual upper design ceilings**: they assume 6,000 canisters simultaneously at the 1,700 W design maximum over 70 Julian years. They are neither observed heat nor lower-bounded flux. COMP23, FARF31, MARFA, release, groundwater-route, exposure, and future-dependency claims must remain model-conditional or regulated-pathway claims. The current legal citation should be the consolidated Swedish SSMFS version including the 2018:19 amendment; the English translation may remain an aid but is not legally binding.[15] [16]

### 2.2 Voyager 1 after a stipulated radio cessation — **historically nonterminal; future scenario model-conditionally nonterminal**

Voyager 1 is empirically nonterminal over its historical and current operational life. The post-radio result survives only as the following conditional scenario: **if** intentional radio transmission has permanently ceased at the stipulated finite date and **if** the spacecraft retains the cited three-MHW-RTG fuel/configuration and decay model, then outbound decay heat remains positive across a spacecraft-only boundary. NASA’s 2026 status establishes continued operations/data return rather than permanent radio cessation; it changes the evidentiary status of the 2035 endpoint without contradicting the decay model.[5] [17] [18]

The design inputs are documented beginning-of-mission facts, not observations of the future craft. Under the deliberately conservative 58-year convention, three times 2,243 W is 6,729 W; the instantaneous modelled power is 4.261322 kW and one-Julian-year integrated energy is 1.3394888 × 10^11 J. The integrated mean is **4.244584 kW**, so the draft’s “at least 4.25 kW average” is too high and must be replaced by 4.2446 kW (or approximately 4.24 kW). The 58-year convention must not be described as the actual launch-to-2035 interval, which is approximately 57.3224 Julian years. All future heat quantities must be labelled **DERIVED / MODELLED / CONDITIONAL**, not observed lower bounds.[17]

The record must not double-count spacecraft-bus radiation as energy additional to the RTG heat witness without an energy partition. Loss of the RTG/fuel would invalidate this heat model but could itself create a distinct matter crossing. Historical plasma and cosmic-ray observations must not be projected as numerical witnesses for the hypothetical 2035 year. The Golden Record material claim requires the JPL mission source rather than the cited contents page. NASA’s current 2026 status must replace stale 2018/2025 claims of present operations; older accounts of the plasma-instrument chronology should be reported as unreconciled descriptions rather than silently harmonized.[5] [17] [18] [19]

### 2.3 GW150914 black-hole/source-system frame — **nonterminal conditional on source and propagation modelling**

GW150914 remains nonterminal for the declared enclosing source-system frame **conditional on the general-relativistic source attribution and propagation model**. The observed evidence is calibrated Hanford and Livingston detector strain and a coincident transient. The source association, source waveform, source energy, propagation, and crossing of the stipulated one-light-year world-tube are **derived/model-inferred**, not observations at that boundary. The human/institutional record is external observational context, not a crossing of the one-light-year source boundary.[6] [7]

The finite-window energy and rate claim must be removed. The arithmetic converting 2.5 solar masses times c² to 4.4679 × 10^47 J and then to about 1.416 × 10^40 W is correct for the draft inputs, but the published total-energy posterior is not shown to be confined to the declared crossing window. Retain a positive derived event witness without a joule or watt lower bound unless the horizon is expanded to contain all emission represented by the source estimate or a primary-source window-limited energy integral is supplied.[6] [7]

The record must disclose rather than combine the original-discovery and current-GWOSC parameter versions, date the NIST 2022 CODATA constants, and state the solar-mass convention. Idealized Hawking power, temperature, and lifetime calculations remain **theoretical/semiclassical conditional calculations**, never observed GW150914 channels. Hawking radiation must not be used as an empirical positive witness.[6] [7] [20] [21] [22]

## 4. Required corrections by cost candidate

### 4.1 Deepwater Horizon

The US$128,000 decision-boundary fee must remain segregated from later spill ledgers; no log-to-loss ratio or downstream allocation is permitted. Gate 3 must be marked **not met** unless a decision-time invoice, cancellation instruction, or comparable contemporaneous record is produced. The Commission’s retrospective recounting of July 2010 testimony does not meet that literal gate. The claim that no primary source supplied linked duration or cost evidence must be removed: the federal court records 10–12 hours, BP testimony of approximately US$128,000 plus US$400,000 Schlumberger time, and Schlumberger’s estimate below US$200,000. The differing estimates must not be merged into one saving.[8] [23]

The JIT’s 2011 no-causation finding and the federal court’s 2014 substantial-cause finding are a material disagreement that must be published. Neither finding proves that the log would certainly have prevented all harms or costs. The National Academies final report supports only narrower, software-conditional top-of-cement inferences; stronger diagnostic language belongs to the interim report. Finally, characterize DOJ’s US$20.8 billion figure as a 2015 announced/proposed civil resolution, judicially approved in 2016, and retain every downstream row as non-additive.[8] [23] [24] [25]

### 4.2 Flint

Flint remains non-qualifying because Gate 3 is absent on the reviewed record. The record must delete or correct the attributed US$4 million reconnection figure: the cited task-force source instead reports US$12 million, US$10.1 million/year, and up to US$1 million/month in return-to-Detroit contexts, none of which is a corrosion-control-treatment (CCT) price. The US$171,000 figure may remain only as LAN engineering-services fees for Flint River plant operation and later KWA conversion, not as CCT cost or avoided cost.[9] [26]

The permit/physical-feed-system assertion cannot be published from a citation that now returns a login page; cite the filed order or omit it. The unsupported US$100–150/day claim remains excluded. “Does not qualify on the current reviewed record” must replace any claim that the case is falsified if a document cannot be produced. Any future calculation requires a decision date, cut-off date, price basis, payer rule, and overlap rule. Do not add the OIG’s historical more-than-US$400 million, Michigan’s live US$448,241,284 dashboard total, the EPA award, or its state match; their dates and scopes differ.[9] [26] [27]

### 4.3 Grenfell

Grenfell must remain **PARTIAL/non-qualifying**. Gate 4 must be labelled “partially evidenced; not met as a control-specific gate,” because the Inquiry’s finding that the realised cassette-form unmodified-PE ACM was primarily responsible for rapid fire spread does not establish that the exact costed zinc comparator was a demonstrated fire-safety control or that retaining it would have prevented all harm and cost. Gate 1 also fails on the present record. The September 2014 Proteus brochure and September 2019 overview conflict on product/fire classification and neither identifies the exact 2014 Grenfell cost baseline; the conflict must be disclosed, not resolved by asserting later A2 material as a 2014 certificate.[10] [28] [29]

The £293,368 Rydon cassette-ACM value-engineering figure is a distinct presentation that may not be combined with Harley’s £419,627/£576,973 or other figures. RBKC expenditures, accounts, settlement, and national remediation amounts must remain unaggregated. Align the declared horizon with the 2023 settlement row or remove it, rename the claimed whole-life boundary as a finite observed category map, and limit crossing language to the documented institutional-cost witness unless additional channel witnesses are sourced.[10] [28]

### 4.4 Boeing 737 MAX

Neither single-input MCAS logic nor the deferred standalone AOA DISAGREE alert qualifies because there is no cited contemporaneous same-control price, saving, or quantified schedule value. The training/certification rationale for MCAS disclosure cannot transfer to either technical control. Rename “sensor redundancy” as **single-input MCAS logic** or **MCAS AOA-input comparison/inhibit logic**, because two physical sensors were installed. The MCAS Gate 1 result is conditional: later implementation does not prove that the exact comparator/inhibit design was available in 2015–16. “Used both inputs” must be qualified because post-correction logic compares/inhibits upon disagreement but may operate on the remaining valid signal after a detected failed circuit.[11] [30] [31]

Cite the Ethiopian report wherever it is used and identify its SKYbrary copy as a secondary host. Cite the Q3-2020 schedule locally. The asserted original decision boundary must be restated as a proposed analytical boundary because Gate 3 leaves the actual ex-ante boundary unobserved. Preserve non-additivity: the US$8.259 billion customer-concession charge, US$5.0 billion forecast, reported 2020–21 abnormal costs, and DOJ resolution differ in accounting date, scope, and commercial overlap.[11] [32] [33]

### 4.5 Challenger

Challenger remains partial and non-qualifying solely because Gate 3 is missing. Correct the statement that no dollar amount arose in post-accident hearing evidence: a hypothetical, unscoped “maybe US$5 [or] US$10 million” was mentioned for extra testing/improvement, but it is not a January 1986 decision cost or adopted rationale. The 53 °F no-go cannot be characterized as proven but-for prevention; retain only the Commission’s qualified decision counterfactual and risk linkage.[12] [34]

Describe GAO’s approximately US$1 billion as 25 **counted post-51-L changes**, not necessarily formal changes, and disclose that GAO excludes the SRM settlement from its table. Disclose CBO’s US$84 million summary versus US$85 million table presentation difference. CBO budget increases, GAO changes, and the reported settlement remain non-additive. The FY1987 frame is a documented fiscal-category horizon, not a total social-cost estimate.[12] [34] [35]

### 4.6 Texas pre-Uri winterization

Texas does not qualify because Gate 2 must be changed from partial to **FAIL—MISSING** and Gate 3 remains missing. SB 1133 documents a reporting-centred regime rather than an identified decision-maker declining a specified winterization measure. The 67% component-protection proposition must be labelled observed causal information plus a **modelled counterfactual reduction**, not observed prevention or proof that an omission caused all downstream costs. The 2021 FERC/NERC recommendation is design at the lowest recorded ambient temperature for the nearest city with historical data, including wind-speed heat loss; it must not be paraphrased as a generic lowest historical temperature requirement.[13] [36]

The US$4.3 billion item is a reported VOLL model with partly disclosed inputs, not an invoice or exactly reproducible total. ERCOT’s Brazos item is a sought recovery of approximately US$1.9 billion in unpaid Uri settlement invoices, not a paid settlement or realised statewide loss. The two figures are non-additive. The record must cite the final FERC/NERC investigation for the causal percentages and distinguish the 2011 statute, 2021 adoption order, and 2022-effective consolidated rule.[13] [36] [37]

## 5. Cross-cutting correction and conflict register

1. **Observed, derived, conditional, planned, and unresolved must not be conflated.** Add a “PLANNED / SCHEDULED” label or use prose for future project quantities. A detector strain is observed; its distant source-boundary implication is derived. Design specifications are documented facts, while a future-system claim derived from them is conditional.
2. **Every boundary and horizon must be explicit.** A finite observation window does not establish a whole-life result; a cost ledger must state dates, price basis, payer scope, and overlap treatment. The GW finite window cannot use an unsliced total-energy posterior.
3. **Preserve source disagreements.** At minimum, publish the 2.3 versus 2.7 million m³ SKB conflict; JIT versus federal-court Deepwater causation conflict; final versus interim National Academies scope difference; 2014 versus 2019 Proteus product evidence conflict; current versus historical NASA Voyager status; original-paper versus updated GWOSC parameter versions; and time-versioned Texas statutory/rule sources.
4. **Retain valid arithmetic but correct its evidentiary use.** The Forsmark 2.3-million/ten-year quotient is not a construction rate; Voyager’s 1.34 × 10^11 J is conditional and its mean rate is 4.244584 kW; the GW mass-energy conversion is not attributable to the finite crossing window; and all validated accounting reconciliations are category-specific rather than summable.
5. **No cross-control or cross-ledger causal calculation is permitted.** This includes Deepwater’s fee versus spill costs, Flint CCT versus source-switch/reconnection/response costs, Grenfell value-engineering versus remediation totals, Boeing technical controls versus programme costs, Challenger no-go versus programme budgets, and Texas winterization versus VOLL/Brazos figures.

## 6. Publication-ready conclusion after required edits

After all listed corrections are made, the defensible synthesis is:

> **No selected asymptote is terminal in its declared frame: Forsmark has observed construction/institutional crossings; Voyager has observed whole-life crossings and a conditional finite-date decay-heat crossing; and GW150914 has an observed detector signal with a conditional, model-inferred source-system crossing. Finite empirical non-detection cannot certify universal all-medium, all-time zero; exact-zero conclusions are model-conditional. None of the six cost candidates clears all five preregistered gates, and no downstream-cost allocation or aggregate loss claim is validated.**
