# WO-3 evidence record: terminal-system accounting boundary

**Status labels.** **OBSERVED** identifies a reported event or measurement. **DERIVED** identifies a conclusion that follows from the protocol, definitions, or stated reasoning. **MODELLED / CONDITIONAL** identifies a result that holds only under declared model premises. **PLANNED** identifies a proposed activity or design feature rather than an event. **UNRESOLVED** identifies a question that the available record does not settle. These labels are not interchangeable.

## Decision

**DERIVED — exact-zero boundary.** No externally reportable, empirical certification of **whole-life, all-medium, exact-zero** crossings is attainable for a real system. A finite study can instead report that no crossing was detected in named channels during a declared horizon, using a stated apparatus and detection bound. An internal recorder may be read after that horizon; that later readout need not itself cross the declared boundary during the earlier horizon. It still cannot establish exact zero, a complete ontology, or terminality for the system's whole life. A method detection limit is itself a method- and confidence-qualified threshold, not proof of absence.[1]

**MODELLED / CONDITIONAL — exact-zero boundary.** Exact zero may be proved within a stipulated model only when the model fixes its boundary, inventory, physical laws, initial conditions, and parameter values. Such a proof concerns that model under those premises; it is not empirical certification that the real system has the stipulated properties.[2] [3]

**DERIVED — ontology.** The six original channel families are an initial inventory, not six scalar rates that can be netted or averaged into a universal-zero verdict. The required result is a boundary- and horizon-indexed profile of gross inbound and outbound crossings, carrier or form, uncertainty, provenance, and causal assumptions. A positive lower-bound witness rejects terminality for its declared frame. A finite non-detection remains a mechanism-, apparatus-, and horizon-qualified bound.[2]

**DERIVED — cost decision.** No supplied candidate clears all five preregistered gates. The result is a **bounded cost null**: no candidate qualifies for selection. This does not show that the controls were harmless, that no downstream losses occurred, or that a cost case could never qualify.

## Ontology and measurement disposition

The protocol defines a crossing as a transfer or causal coupling across a declared boundary that changes, constrains, informs, burdens, or exposes something outside the system, or changes the system from outside.[2] The following corrections preserve the six families while preventing a scalar, net-flow interpretation.

| Channel family | Evidence-status disposition | Required profile treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Matter and material transfer | **DERIVED:** Net mass can be zero while species, isotope, phase, size-bin, or counter-transfer crossings are positive. In multicomponent transport, constraints on the sum do not establish that every component is zero.[4] | Record gross inbound and outbound mass and count vectors by carrier, species, isotope, phase, and size bin. Declare the moving, fixed, or porous boundary, reference velocity, capture efficiency, and detection limits. Include permeation, outgassing, films, pores, abrasion, and material charge carriers. |
| Energy, momentum, and fields | **DERIVED:** Energy, linear momentum or impulse, angular momentum or torque, radiation, and quasistatic field constraint are distinct measurands. Near-field radiation demonstrates one experimentally accessible mechanism; it is not a substitute for the other ledgers.[5] | Keep separate ledgers for energy; force, impulse, and linear momentum; torque and angular momentum; radiation by species or mode, including evanescent modes; and static or standing field and boundary-condition coupling. Entropy and exergy are derived diagnostics, not carriers. |
| Information, observation, and control | **DERIVED:** An event count alone loses the roles of probe, readout, disturbance, command, persistent set-point, causal direction, and shared history. Correlation is not a directed transfer without explicit causal assumptions.[6] | Distinguish probe input, readout output, measurement back-action, commands or actuation, persistent authority, set-points, and interlocks. Retain endpoint, direction, code, and record provenance with the causal-identification assumptions. |
| Biological | **DERIVED:** The original list is incomplete if it omits virions, infectious proteins, extracellular vesicles and bioactive cargo, extracellular nucleic acids or mobile elements, and nonviable assay-confounding signatures. Functional extracellular-vesicle RNA delivery is one reason not to reduce biological crossing to organism count alone.[7] | Use a non-exclusive profile for cells and propagules; virions; infectious proteins; extracellular vesicles and bioactive cargo; extracellular nucleic acids and mobile elements; and nonviable signatures. State recovery and assay sensitivity, together with establishment, persistence, infectivity, transformation, or receptor effect where relevant. |
| Human, legal, financial, and institutional | **DERIVED:** Event flows can omit continuing licences, duties, guarantees, authority, membership, and contingent claims. A present stand-ready obligation need not produce a payment during the horizon.[8] | Record relational stocks as well as events: perimeter or jurisdiction, active relation count, relation-days, exposure, authority, and relation changes. Cross-tag implicit support or legitimacy rather than silently recasting it as cash or labour. |
| Dependency, exposure, and causal consequence | **DERIVED:** Recipient counts and realised inbound or outbound quantities can omit inbound dependence, common cause, co-location or shared hazards, logical or governance links, and static coupling. Logical interdependencies may arise from rules and decisions rather than a physical or cyber flow.[9] | Record declared dependency, shared-cause, spatial, logical or governance, static-state or field, and counterfactual-exposure edges or conditions. Treat consequences as **MODELLED / CONDITIONAL** unless activation is observed; report substitutability, latency, reversibility, and conditional severity. |

**PLANNED — common measurement rules.** Every profile component should declare the evolving boundary and inclusion convention; horizon and sampling coverage; gross rather than netted inbound and outbound quantities; carrier or form and endpoints; instrument and procedure; calibration; recovery or capture efficiency; threshold and uncertainty; provenance; and causal assumptions. It should identify its own status as observed, derived, modelled or conditional, planned, or unresolved.[2]

### Overlap control and exact-zero language

**DERIVED — no averaging rule.** The six audits do not yield competing scalar estimates to average. A beta electron, ion, neutron, or alpha particle can be one material carrier that also transports energy and momentum. It must be represented as one event with linked matter and energy-momentum ledgers; summing the ledgers would double-count it. A licence, veto, interlock, or set-point can simultaneously be an institutional relation, dependency, and control state without a new information event. A physical static field or constraint and the dependency edge it supports must likewise receive separate, shared-identifier records. Biological signatures remain material transfers, but their biological significance also depends on infectivity, transformation, establishment, or receptor effect.

**DERIVED — exact-zero language.** A scoped non-detection, a conditional deductive proof, and empirical terminality certification are different claims. The first reports finite evidence under an apparatus and detection bound. The second is valid only under a model's stipulated premises. Neither independently certifies that every possible crossing is exactly zero for all time in the real system.[1] [2]

## Asymptote cases

The classifications below are deliberately frame-specific. A positive **OBSERVED** witness is sufficient to reject terminality for the declared boundary and horizon. A **MODELLED / CONDITIONAL** witness supports only the frame defined by its model. None of the three cases supplies empirical, whole-life, all-medium exact-zero certification.

| Case and declared frame | Validated evidence and classification | Boundary and remaining limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Forsmark KBS-3 repository** — whole-life accounting frame that includes construction and institutional control | **OBSERVED — nonterminal.** Ground-breaking and preparatory surface works began in January 2025, and regulatory and reporting interfaces already exist. These witnesses suffice; classification does not require observed underground excavation, heat flow, or post-closure release.[10] **PLANNED / UNRESOLVED — volume conflict.** The principal SKB project page, reviewed 17 June 2025, says approximately 2.7 million m³ will be extracted. An SKB International page, reviewed 25 March 2026, says approximately 2.3 million m³. Both figures are planned, not already excavated.[10] [11] | Do not silently select 2.3 or 2.7 million m³. The sources agree on construction status and broad configuration, but their planned volumes conflict. The International page says construction takes about ten years while rock excavation occurs over several decades and tunnels are built in parallel with deposition; therefore no ten-year excavation rate is established. The 1,700-W-per-canister value is a design constraint, not a measured lower-bound heat flux.[11] [12] |
| **Voyager 1 projected post-radio phase** — moving spacecraft boundary; illustrative horizon from a stipulated 2035 cessation date through 2036 | **OBSERVED — current context.** On 17 April 2026 NASA reported that Voyager 1 was still sending data after an instrument shutdown, so permanent radio cessation in 2035 is not an observed condition.[13] **MODELLED / CONDITIONAL — nonterminal in the stated model.** Given three RTGs, a beginning-of-mission 2,243-W thermal specification for each, intact RTGs, the stated decay scaling, radio cessation, and radiation of rejected heat, the calculation is approximately \(3 \times 2{,}243 = 6{,}729\) W initially, \(6{,}729 \times 2^{-58/88} \approx 4.26\) kW in 2035, and \(1.34 \times 10^{11}\) J over one Julian year. This is a positive thermal crossing at every finite date within that model.[14] [15] | The quantity is not 2035 telemetry and not an observed lower bound for the real spacecraft. It depends on RTG inventory, integrity, decay scaling, the cessation date, and thermal behaviour. The Golden Record remains embodied information inside the moving boundary unless it is transferred. |
| **GW150914 black-hole system** — external merger system enclosed by a stipulated one-light-year accounting world-tube | **OBSERVED — external signal.** LIGO detectors observed strain associated with GW150914 at Hanford and Livingston.[16] **MODELLED / CONDITIONAL — nonterminal.** The published general-relativistic source-model posterior gives radiated energy of \(3.0^{+0.5}_{-0.5}\,M_\odot c^2\). Converting its 90%-credible lower endpoint gives \(2.5\,M_\odot c^2 = 4.4679 \times 10^{47}\) J. Propagation of that inferred radiation through the stipulated world-tube is model-inferred, not a direct measurement on that invented surface.[17] | The event horizon is an internal causal boundary, not the accounting boundary around the external system. Classical one-way horizon structure does not negate the observed exterior waveform or the conditional source-energy inference. **MODELLED / CONDITIONAL (theoretical):** Hawking radiation is a semiclassical prediction, not an observed channel from GW150914, and is not used as the positive witness.[18] |

**DERIVED — asymptote conclusion.** Forsmark is nonterminal on an observed construction witness in its declared whole-life frame. The future Voyager phase is nonterminal only under the stated intact-RTG decay model, while its proposed cessation remains unobserved. GW150914 has an observed external detector signal and a model-inferred energy crossing at the stipulated world-tube; it is not a model-free boundary measurement. These distinctions resolve the apparent black-hole tension without collapsing an internal event horizon into the full system boundary.

## Strict five-gate cost-case screen

The preregistered gates are: **G1**, a specific control was available; **G2**, a decision-maker declined, removed, deferred, or weakened it; **G3**, contemporaneous evidence gives its ex-ante cost, saving, schedule value, or explicit cost rationale; **G4**, an authoritative investigation connects the omitted control to later harm or risk; and **G5**, an authoritative or methodologically transparent record supports a downstream cost category.[2] In the table, **P** means supported in the available record, **F** means not met, and **U** means not established for a decision-specific, non-overlapping redraw. A single F or U prevents qualification.

| Candidate | G1 | G2 | G3 | G4 | G5 | Strict result |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|---|
| Deepwater Horizon cement-evaluation log | P | P | F | P* | P | **Does not qualify.** Gate 3 lacks the required decision-time cost record. Gate 4 is materially disputed: the 2011 Joint Investigation Team found no log-specific causation, while a 2014 federal court found the omission a substantial cause. Neither finding supports allocating later costs to the cancelled log.[19] |
| Flint corrosion control | P | P | F | P | P | **Does not qualify.** The causal record supports prolonged lead exposure, but the available record does not provide a contemporaneous control-specific ex-ante cost, saving, schedule value, or explicit cost rationale. Plant, source-switch, or later-response figures cannot replace it.[20] |
| Grenfell zinc-to-PE-ACM substitution | F | P | P | F | P | **Does not qualify.** The £293,368 value-engineering figure and the authoritative rapid-spread finding do not establish the fire status of the exact costed zinc baseline or a control-specific prevention counterfactual. No saving-to-loss ratio or assignment of recovery or settlement costs to this substitution is supported.[21] |
| Boeing 737 MAX MCAS input logic/AOA alert | U | P | F | P | P | **Does not qualify.** Availability of the exact MCAS comparator or inhibit design at the decision point remains conditional, and neither technical control has a same-control contemporaneous cost, saving, or quantified schedule value. Program-level MAX charges are not transferred to either control.[22] |
| Challenger temperature no-go | P | P | F | P | P | **Does not qualify.** The launch decision and causal field-joint failure are documented, but no decision-specific scrub, delay, reversal cost, saving, or quantified schedule benefit is established. Post-accident appropriations and redesign costs cannot backfill the missing original value.[23] |
| Texas pre-2021 winterization | P | F | F | P | P | **Does not qualify.** Available controls and multi-cause risk evidence are documented, but the record does not identify an actor's decision to decline a defined control or a contemporaneous decision-specific cost or rationale. Retrospective plant estimates and aggregate Uri losses are not a qualifying comparison.[24] |

**DERIVED — bounded cost null.** Each case is retained as a documented near-miss, but none is selected. The screen may be reopened only when primary or official contemporaneous evidence closes every gate, including a transparent causal basis for a decision-specific redraw. The result is a limitation of the supplied evidence, not a statement that all omitted controls had zero consequence.

`P*` means that at least one authoritative source makes the required connection while another authoritative source materially conflicts with it. `U` means that the gate is not established for the exact control as framed.

**DERIVED — non-additivity rule.** The original decision-boundary cost, added direct organisational cost, transferred public or third-party cost, long-horizon liability, and unmonetized harm are distinct, **non-additive** categories. They must not be summed across scopes, attributed to an omitted control without an authoritative or transparent counterfactual allocation, or converted into a saving-to-loss ratio. No aggregate downstream loss, allocation, or ratio is reported in this record.

## Scope and next evidence needed

**UNRESOLVED — candidate universe.** The three asymptotes are not an exhaustive search for terminal systems. They show that these named cases do not supply a terminal instance in their declared frames; they do not prove that no possible system is terminal.

**UNRESOLVED — contamination marker.** Human-built material and designed systems may reach another world, but this record does not determine whether contamination occurs. The possibility remains an open channel, not a finding in either direction.

**PLANNED — next tests.** A subsequent study should attack the crossing inventory by naming omitted media or boundary ambiguities; publish a profile with explicit bounds for each channel; test the three asymptotes under declared boundaries and horizons; and search decision records for a case that closes all five cost gates. It must report source versions where they conflict, rather than resolving them by unstated selection.
