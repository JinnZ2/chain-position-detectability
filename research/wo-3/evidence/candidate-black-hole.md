# WO-3 Evidence Record — GW150914 Black-Hole Source System

**Case ID:** `WO-3-BH-GW150914`
**Status:** Corrected evidence record
**Candidate selection:** This record assesses **one candidate only**: the inferred GW150914 binary-black-hole source system and post-merger remnant, bounded outside its horizons. It does not assess Voyager or a geological repository.
**Metric:** WO-3 crossing-rate metric [1].
**Decision:** **nonterminal — model-conditional physical classification**.

## Corrected finding

**OBSERVED:** On 14 September 2015, LIGO Hanford and Livingston recorded a coincident calibrated strain transient. The discovery analysis reports a matched-filter signal-to-noise ratio of 24, a false-alarm rate below one event per 203,000 years, significance greater than \(5.1\sigma\), a frequency sweep from 35 to 250 Hz, and peak strain of \(1.0\times10^{-21}\). These are detector-level observations, subject to the calibration and analysis described by the collaboration. [2] [3]

**DERIVED:** Conditional on the supported general-relativistic waveform, cosmological, source-association, and propagation models, the transient was emitted by the inferred GW150914 binary-black-hole source and propagated outward through every enclosing exterior surface. That includes the declared one-light-year accounting surface. This is a derived physical crossing supported by an observed detector signal; it is **not** a direct observation of the source, either event-horizon interior, or the one-light-year surface. On that explicitly conditional basis, at least one source-to-exterior crossing event is positive and the candidate is nonterminal under the metric. [1] [2]

No assertion here is model-free. The empirical certification is limited to the detector signal and its local instrument interaction. The source-system classification follows only from the stated physical inference.

## Declared frame and scope

### System and accounting boundary

**DERIVED:** \(S\) is the inferred astrophysical source of GW150914: the two compact objects inferred to be black holes before merger, their event-horizon interiors, the exterior strong-field and near-zone system, and the post-merger remnant. The system is not an observed image or sample of an event-horizon interior. [2]

**MODELLED / CONDITIONAL:** \(B_{1\,ly}\) is an approximately spherical, source-frame timelike world-tube with areal radius one light-year, centered on an inferred source barycentre. It encloses the horizons and exterior source system and excludes remote systems, including detectors. This is a conventional accounting surface, not a physical wall or an observed geometric object. Its coordinate and model dependence is retained deliberately. Conditional on outward propagation from the inferred source, a gravitational-wave signal from merger reaches this surface about one source-frame year after emission.

### Horizons and lifecycle

**DERIVED:** The finite crossing horizon is

\[
H_{cross}=[t_m-10\ \mathrm{s},\;t_m+1\ \mathrm{source\ year}+10\ \mathrm{s}],
\]

where \(t_m\) is the inferred source-frame merger time. It is a finite event-assessment window, not a complete lifecycle. It is useful for recording that a model-inferred outward propagation event would cross \(B_{1\,ly}\), but it does not support a window-specific radiated-energy total or average rate.

**MODELLED / CONDITIONAL:** The whole-life horizon \(H_{WL}\) begins with formation of the first black-hole horizon and extends through afterlife. Its endpoint is open. In classical general relativity a remnant has no specified endpoint; in the semiclassical Hawking model an isolated remnant eventually evaporates. A finite average rate over this open interval is not reported. Under the preregistered rule, the positive derived event in \(H_{cross}\subset H_{WL}\) is sufficient to reject whole-life terminality for this declared source-system frame. [1]

## Evidence and metric witness

The primary analysis reports source-model estimates of radiated gravitational-wave energy of \(3.0^{+0.5}_{-0.5}\,M_\odot c^2\), with 90% credible intervals. This remains useful **DERIVED** information about the modelled source. It is not an observation of energy at \(B_{1\,ly}\), and the cited record does not allocate the full posterior interval to the source-time interval whose radiation would cross the declared finite \(H_{cross}\). Therefore this evidence record reports **no** \(Q_{energy,out}(B_{1\,ly},H_{cross})\) lower bound in joules and **no** average rate in watts. In particular, it does not use \(2.5\,M_\odot c^2\), \(4.47\times10^{47}\) J, or \(1.42\times10^{40}\) W as a bound or rate for this finite window. [2]

## Source-parameter versions and retained conflict

The record preserves rather than combines two source versions. The original discovery paper reports source-frame component masses of \(36^{+5}_{-4}\,M_\odot\) and \(29^{+4}_{-4}\,M_\odot\), final mass \(62^{+4}_{-4}\,M_\odot\), and radiated energy \(3.0^{+0.5}_{-0.5}\,M_\odot c^2\), with 90% credible intervals. [2] The current GWOSC event page states that its parameter table was updated using a later analysis and reports \(36.2^{+5.2}_{-3.8}\,M_\odot\), \(29.1^{+3.7}_{-4.4}\,M_\odot\), final mass \(62.3^{+3.7}_{-3.1}\,M_\odot\), and remnant spin \(0.68^{+0.05}_{-0.06}\). It retains the same radiated-energy central estimate and interval and notes a 15 May 2019 correction of the radiated-energy uncertainty to \(\pm0.5\). [3]

**UNRESOLVED:** These are version-specific posterior products, not direct interior measurements. This record does not combine the original-paper mass values with the later GWOSC spin or mass envelope in any calculation. It makes no Hawking numerical estimate, so the source-version difference has no hidden numerical role.

## Horizon ontology and asymptote boundary

### Event horizon is not the source-system boundary

**MODELLED / CONDITIONAL:** In ideal classical general relativity, an event horizon is one-way: an outward signal launched from within the horizon is not predicted to emerge through it. This is a narrow statement about a specified classical model and an outbound direction. It is not an observation, does not imply zero inbound crossings, and does not imply that the binary/remnant-plus-exterior source system has zero crossings. [4]

The **only exact-zero statement retained** in this record is therefore narrow: *within the ideal classical model, an outward signal that begins inside an event horizon has zero predicted outward passage through that horizon.* It is not an empirical exact-zero measurement; it says nothing about inbound matter, radiation, momentum, or information, and it does not apply to the enclosing \(B_{1\,ly}\) source-system boundary.

**UNRESOLVED:** For the strict interior subsystem with its horizon as a boundary, this record has no source-specific quantitative inventory of inbound crossings during formation or merger. Its status is not terminal. The one-way classical proposition cannot establish all-channel zero, and the remote observations cannot empirically certify a zero upper bound for every possible channel.

### Validated asymptote classification

The work order lists “black hole interior past the horizon” as an asymptote rather than an established terminal instance. This evidence record preserves that discipline. **Nonterminal** is assigned only to the **inferred GW150914 source system enclosed by \(B_{1\,ly}\)** and only as a **MODELLED / CONDITIONAL** physical classification based on the gravitational-wave source attribution and propagation model. It is not assigned to an event-horizon interior merely because of the detector measurement, and it is not a universal claim about all black holes or all boundaries. [1] [2]

**OBSERVED:** The detectors recorded strain; they did not observe a one-light-year crossing, a horizon interior, a Hawking flux, a formation event, or an ultimate endpoint. **DERIVED:** With the stipulated physical model, the detector signal supports the source-to-exterior propagation and therefore the enclosing-boundary crossing. **UNRESOLVED:** A remote source's all-channel exact-zero status cannot be settled by the detector record alone. Absence of a detected channel is never treated as an upper bound of zero. [1]

## Formation, afterlife, and theoretical channels

**UNRESOLVED — formation:** The event analysis constrains source parameters near merger but does not reconstruct this binary's earlier formation history. This record does not assert a progenitor, formation mass, ejected mass, formation energy, or formation channel. Classical collapse or accretion could involve inbound crossings, but their source-specific values are not established here. [2]

**DERIVED — merger and operation:** The late inspiral, merger, and ringdown waveform is interpreted as a general-relativistic binary-black-hole signal. The reported total radiated-energy posterior applies to the source model, but not to a supported energy allocation within \(H_{cross}\). The signal event, rather than an invented finite-window energy integral, is the retained witness. [2]

**MODELLED / CONDITIONAL — accretion and collision:** Accretion theory generally permits inward matter flow and outward radiation in black-hole environments. This is not evidence of an accretion disk, electromagnetic luminosity, ejecta, or a positive accretion rate for GW150914. Future accretion, material encounter, or merger is an open stochastic possibility without an assigned probability or timescale in this record. [7]

**MODELLED / CONDITIONAL — Hawking radiation:** Hawking's semiclassical calculation predicts particle emission, mass loss, and eventual disappearance for black holes; Page's calculation shows that rotating-hole emission and lifetime depend on spin. These are theoretical results. They are not a detected Hawking flux from GW150914 and are not used as an observed or derived lower bound in this record. [5] [6]

**UNRESOLVED:** Information recovery, firewalls, remnants, wormholes, and nonstandard quantum-gravity escape mechanisms are excluded from the witness. So are biological transfer and intentional messaging. None is needed to sustain, or can negate, the bounded conditional result.

## Decision, limitations, and planned resolution

**Decision:** `nonterminal — model-conditional physical classification`. The derived positive source-to-exterior propagation event rejects terminality for the declared inferred source-system frame, conditional on the LIGO/Virgo general-relativistic source attribution and propagation model. The result does not depend on Hawking radiation, a reconstructed formation history, an accretion estimate, or a future-collision probability. [1] [2]

**UNRESOLVED:** The local environment, formation history, total earlier inspiral energy, future interactions, exact external dependency count, biological channels, and speculative quantum-gravity channels remain unresolved. No non-detection becomes \(U=0\). The finite \(H_{cross}\) retains no numerical energy or rate lower bound because the cited total radiated energy is not allocated to that window.

**PLANNED:** A future revision may report a finite-window energy quantity only if a primary or methodologically transparent source supplies an energy integral confined to the emission epochs represented by \(H_{cross}\), or if the declared horizon is changed to match the full supported radiated-energy estimate. It may investigate additional channels only with source-specific evidence and published assumptions. Any future cost assessment must independently pass all five selection gates before costs are categorized, allocated, or compared.
