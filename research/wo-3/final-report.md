# WO-3 Final Report: The Terminal System as an Accounting Boundary

**Author:** Manus AI
**Research date:** 19 September 2026
**Status:** Validated first milestone complete

## Executive finding

The first WO-3 milestone did **not** find a terminal physical or engineered system among the three named asymptotes. Forsmark is nonterminal within its declared whole-life frame on observed preparatory construction and institutional crossings. Voyager 1 is observed nonterminal over its historical life; its future post-radio phase is nonterminal only under a stated, model-conditional radioisotope-heat calculation. The GW150914 source system is nonterminal only conditional on the general-relativistic source-attribution and propagation model; the detector strain is observed, but the stipulated source boundary was not.[1] [2]

The work order's falsifier remains logically valid: a system with zero crossings in every medium would refute the universal claim that no terminal system exists. The empirical version cannot certify **whole-life, all-medium, exact zero**. A finite test can detect a crossing or report that no crossing was detected in named channels above stated limits. It cannot demonstrate that its inventory is complete or that every possible crossing remains exactly zero for all time. Exact-zero conclusions are therefore model-conditional, not model-free empirical findings.[2] [3] [4]

The proposed cost-boundary demonstration also returned a result, but not the expected case study. Six prominent candidates were screened under five preregistered gates. **None of the six cost candidates qualifies.** Every candidate lacked at least one required link between a defined control, a documented decision, a contemporaneous same-control cost or rationale, an authoritative harm or risk connection, and a reproducible downstream-cost category. The correct publication result is a **bounded selection null**, not a forced comparison.[2]

## The crossing-rate instrument became a profile

The preregistered measure used gross inbound and outbound quantities rather than net flows:

`Rm(B,H) = [Qm,in(B,H) + Qm,out(B,H)] / duration(H)`.

That definition survives, but the six medium audits showed that the result cannot be six scalar rows. Matter, joules, torque, bits, licences, and dependent-hours have no natural common unit. A system must instead receive a boundary- and horizon-indexed profile. Each event records its carrier, direction, endpoints, gross quantity interval, evidence status, uncertainty, provenance, causal status, and links to other ledgers.[2] [3]

The matter ledger must preserve constituent counterflows. The energy ledger must distinguish energy, linear momentum, angular momentum, radiation mode, and static field coupling. Information records must distinguish probe, readout, back-action, commands, and standing control. Biological records must include virions, infectious proteins, extracellular vesicles, mobile genetic elements, and nonviable signatures. Institutional records must include standing relations such as licences and guarantees. Dependency records must represent inbound dependence, common causes, co-location, governance, and static coupling.[3]

This structure prevents two opposite errors. Netting can hide positive crossings, while naïvely summing linked ledgers can double-count one event. The amended profile therefore keeps one event identity with several linked measurements rather than producing a universal scalar score.

## The falsifier survives only as a model-level test

The six independent medium audits all rejected empirical certification of literal all-medium zero. That agreement must be stated narrowly. It does not prove that a terminal physical system is logically impossible. It shows that finite empirical work produces detections or bounded non-detections, not universal exact-zero certification.

An internal recorder illustrates the distinction. It can remain within the boundary during a finite horizon and be read later. The resulting record may support the statement that no specified crossing was detected during that interval above stated thresholds. The later readout does not retroactively create a crossing during the earlier interval. Even so, the record cannot establish that the channel inventory was complete, that every detection threshold was zero, or that no crossing will occur after the horizon. A formal proof can establish exact zero only within stipulated laws, parameters, initial conditions, and boundary definitions.[3] [4]

The operational classification is therefore:

- **Observed nonterminal within frame** when at least one observed crossing or external relation has a positive lower bound.
- **Model-conditionally nonterminal within frame** when the positive witness follows only from declared model premises.
- **Unresolved** when no positive witness is established but at least one upper bound is open or the inventory is incomplete.
- **Terminal within a stipulated model** only when every enumerated channel and dependency has an upper bound of zero under that model.

No reviewed candidate reached the last category.

| GW150914 black-hole source system | **Model-conditionally nonterminal** | Hanford and Livingston observed a coincident strain signal. These cases fail for different reasons. Forsmark fails terminality before post-closure modelling matters. Voyager shows that radio silence eliminates one information channel, not thermal radiation or its earlier lifecycle. The black-hole case turns on boundary discipline: a classical event horizon is an internal causal boundary, while the scored source system includes exterior dynamics and a model-inferred outgoing gravitational-wave signal.

The contamination marker remains **UNKNOWN**. A human-built object may eventually transfer matter or designed information to another environment, but the reviewed record establishes neither a collision nor a biologically relevant transfer. The open possibility cannot be promoted to a positive finding or collapsed to zero.[1]

## Six cost candidates produced a bounded null

The cost study required all five gates. A candidate had to identify a specific control, document the decision to weaken or decline it, provide contemporaneous same-control cost or schedule evidence, connect the omission to later harm or risk through an authoritative investigation, and provide at least one reproducible downstream-cost category.

| Candidate | G1 | G2 | G3 | G4 | G5 | Result |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|---|
| Deepwater Horizon cement-evaluation log | Pass | Pass | **Fail** | Pass, disputed | Pass | Does not qualify. The official record conflicts on causation, and no decision-time cost document was located.[12] [13] |
| Flint corrosion-control treatment | Pass | Pass | **Fail** | Pass, qualified | Pass | Does not qualify. No contemporaneous control-specific cost, saving, schedule value, or rationale was found.[14] |
| Grenfell zinc-to-PE-ACM substitution | **Fail** | Pass | Pass | **Fail** | Pass | Does not qualify. The exact costed zinc baseline is not proven to be the omitted fire-safety control, and the required control-specific counterfactual is absent.[15] |
| Boeing 737 MAX MCAS input logic and AOA alert | Conditional | Pass | **Fail** | Pass | Pass | Does not qualify. No same-control contemporaneous cost or quantified schedule value exists in the reviewed record.[16] |
| Challenger O-ring launch no-go | Pass | Pass | **Fail** | Pass, limited | Pass | Does not qualify. The decision and risk link are documented, but no decision-specific scrub or delay value was found.[17] |
| Texas generation winterization | Pass | **Fail** | **Fail** | Pass, mixed observed/modelled | Pass, modelled | Does not qualify. The record lacks both an identified actor's declined-control decision and a contemporaneous decision-specific cost or rationale.[18] |

The Deepwater case shows why strict gates matter. A later official report recorded a US$128,000 fee, but the decision-time cost record was not located. More importantly, the 2011 Joint Investigation Team found that omission of the log was not a cause of the well failure, while a 2014 federal court found it a substantial cause. The conflict must remain visible; neither finding licenses an allocation of later spill costs to the log decision.[12] [13]

Grenfell came closest to the intended boundary redraw because a £293,368 value-engineering figure and the Inquiry's rapid-spread finding are documented. It still failed the exact-control test. The available record does not prove that Rydon's costed zinc baseline was the same noncombustible option described elsewhere, nor that retaining that exact option would have prevented the losses. Comparing £293,368 with response, settlement, or remediation totals would therefore create the causal allocation that the evidence does not supply.[15]

The null is informative. It supports the work order's observation that honest decision-level comparisons are often missing or incomplete. It does **not** prove that cost never matters, that the six controls had no effects, or that no organization possesses a qualifying record. It shows that public retrospective totals cannot substitute for the original decision comparison.

## Cost-boundary rule after the screen

A valid boundary redraw must preserve distinct categories instead of summing them automatically. The original decision-boundary cost, direct organizational consequences, transferred public or third-party costs, long-horizon liabilities, and unmonetized harms may have different payers, dates, legal scopes, and overlaps. A downstream figure may be real without being attributable to one control.

No avoided-loss total, net-cost delta, or saving-to-loss ratio is reported for WO-3. Reopening a candidate requires a contemporaneous same-control decision record and a transparent causal allocation. If those exist, the analysis must retain original currency and year, state the payer and time horizon, reconcile overlaps, and leave death, injury, or trauma unmonetized unless the original decision itself used a necessary and explicit valuation.

## Conclusion

WO-3's strongest validated result is methodological. Apparent terminality is highly sensitive to the accounting boundary, lifecycle horizon, channel inventory, and evidence status. The three named asymptotes did not supply a terminal instance, but that failure does not prove that terminal systems are impossible. It shows that the universal empirical claim is stronger than finite observation can support.

The companion cost study reached the same discipline from another direction. Six plausible examples contained fragments of the desired comparison, but none supplied the complete decision-to-cost-to-harm chain required for a causal redraw. The publishable result is therefore two bounded findings: **no terminal instance among the three tested asymptotes, and no qualifying cost case among the six screened candidates**.
